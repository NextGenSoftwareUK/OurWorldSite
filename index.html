THE VISION

A NEW KIND OF GAME

Our World is not just a game — it's a movement. Built on the OASIS platform and powered by OGEngine, it overlays a rich digital world onto the real one using augmented and virtual reality, anchored to real GPS coordinates around the globe.

Every location you visit, every positive action you take — planting trees, helping neighbours, volunteering, reducing waste — earns Karma. Karma is your currency, your reputation, and your power in Our World. As it grows it raises your max health, armour, mana, stamina, ammo and movement speed — and at high karma levels unlocks superpowers: flight, telekinesis, teleportation, super speed, super strength and more — that carry across every OASIS game.

Quests, achievements and inventory are shared across the entire OASIS Omniverse via OGEngine — complete a mission in ODOOM, carry the rewards into Our World. One unified identity. One interconnected universe.

THE EARLY YEARS

DESIGNED SINCE 2011

Our World didn't appear overnight. It's the result of over a decade of late-night vision documents, napkin sketches and relentless dreaming — long before a single line of OASIS code was written.

📓
2011
THE FIRST NOTES

The very first ideas for Our World were written down — a real-world GPS AR game that rewards good deeds with karma and turns every park, street and open space into living game content. The vision was always this big.

💡
2013–2015
THE IDEAS FILES

Hundreds of pages of design documents: vibration mechanics, AR combat, character classes, clan bases in parks, spiritual tech trees, NFC jewellery that gives your avatar stat bonuses, real-world delivery tracking on the game map, and more. All of it written before a single line of engine code existed.

🏗️
2016–PRESENT
THE BUILD BEGINS

The OASIS platform starts taking shape. Ten years of solo engineering — no team, no funding, no blueprint. Building every library, network layer and storage provider by hand, all to give Our World the backbone it deserves.

DAVID ELLAMS — CREATOR

"I've been designing Our World since 2011 — writing vision documents, filling notebooks, dreaming up mechanics that the technology of the day couldn't even run. Every park quest, every karma loop, every spiritual tech tree idea was written down years before the engine existed. OASIS was built to make all of this possible. The wait is finally over."

GAME DESIGN

DEEP MECHANICS

Our World runs on systems designed over a decade before the engine existed. This is what's under the hood.

⚡
VIBRATION SYSTEM

Every area of the real world has a live vibration level, visible on your AR map as a dark-to-light gradient. Walking through a park raises the vibration; litter and pollution lower it. The global vibration bar is the collective state of every player on earth — if it falls too low, the dark wins and the nukes fly. If it rises high enough, new dimensions unlock.

🌿
LIVING NATURE

Plant trees and they grow over time, gaining health and strength. When they're tall enough they defend themselves — swatting dark agents with their branches. The more trees you plant, the more powerful the network they form. Hug a tree in AR to recharge faster. Neglect them and they weaken. Nature is alive and it responds to your care.

👹
DARK AGENTS & BOSSES

NPC Dark Agents roam the map lowering vibration, dropping litter and erecting Dark Transmitters. Use your Light Beam to convert them — tap for rapid fire, hold to charge a big shot. If an area's vibration drops too low a giant Dark Boss spawns requiring multiple players to defeat, with visible weak points that reward co-ordinated teamwork.

🏰
CLAN BASES IN PARKS

Join or form a clan — Elf, Dwarf, Human, Fairy, Wizard — each with its own tech tree and unique structures. Build your clan base in a real local park, defend it from NPC attack waves, and control territory. Clans don't fight each other — only the dark forces. Tournaments, leagues and seasonal events run across all parks worldwide.

🔮
SPIRITUAL CARD TRADING

Discover spiritual cards hidden in local parks — the universe "randomly" selects the cards closest to you, like a real-time AR tarot reading. Meditate with your card of the day using an EEG headband and earn karma for calmness. Each card grants your avatar special powers for quests and Training Centre battles. Some cards can be traded; others were chosen only for you.

💍
NFC REAL-WORLD ITEMS

Wear real NFC-chipped jewellery — rings, amulets, pendants — scan them with your phone and they appear on your avatar with matching stat bonuses. A ring becomes +10 health. An orgone pendant transmutes dark energy and boosts your light beam. The physical and digital world are literally the same world.

🌀
144 PORTALS OF LIGHT

Activate all 144 Portals of Light scattered across the real world by gathering players to meditate and send their light beams into them. Each portal slowly closes without ongoing collective attention — keeping them open is a shared responsibility. Fully activating all 144 triggers a planetary light grid event and unlocks hidden dimensions for every player on earth.

🚀
ASCENSION & BEYOND

Raise your vibration high enough and you begin to ascend — unlocking flight, telekinesis, teleportation, personal spaceships and eventually the ability to terraform planets, build solar systems and create entire universes. Our World is an ascension training programme. The journey from primitive human to co-creator of reality — that's the endgame.

🌦️
THE WORLD'S MOOD IS THE WEATHER

The sky in Our World reflects the collective emotional state of everyone playing. When the community is happy and helping each other, it's sunny. When people are struggling, clouds gather and storms roll in. You can literally make it a brighter day — by helping someone feel better.

🌙
THE DREAM REALM

When your character sleeps at night, you enter the Dream Realm — where you can fly, try out powers before committing to them, and explore impossible places. Stay up too late and your character suffers: fatigue, reduced stamina, health decline. Rest well and you wake stronger.

👑
THE CHAKRA TECH TREE

Seven chakras. Seven skill trees. Collect energy, reach thresholds, choose new abilities. Like Diablo's skill trees — but rooted in genuine spiritual tradition. Crown chakra unlocks summoning archangels. Third Eye unlocks the ability to see everyone's energy and aura.

😇
SUMMON ARCHANGELS

Advance far enough in the Crown chakra tree and you can summon archangels — Archangel Michael for protection, Raphael for healing and chakra restoration. Inspired by Final Fantasy's summon system but drawing on real spiritual tradition.

💎
CRYSTALS & SACRED GEOMETRY

Collect chakra gems (emeralds for the heart, amethysts for the crown), crystal skulls and fragments of sacred geometry — Flower of Life, Metatron's Cube and more. Bring complete sets to the Sacred Geometry Temple to unlock unique powers unavailable anywhere else.

⚔️
COMBAT ZONES

Themed combat zones — Terminator zones, zombie mall sieges, village defence waves. Robots, zombies and undead only — no humans harmed, no aliens. Classic or Spiritual mode. Build defences with neighbours. Player duels only in the arena — angels revive both fighters at the end.

🏡
BUILD YOUR WORLD

Own land, build homes and shops from whatever materials you choose — including Playdoh and food (the witch's house is possible). Ask the nature spirits permission first. Replace what you use. Real companies can open virtual shops, sell real products and deliver them to your door.

👁️
SEE ENERGY

Unlock the Third Eye ability to toggle your view and see everyone's chakras, auras and energy flows lit up around them. A visual light festival of the soul — visible only to those who have earned the gift through genuine spiritual progression.

🛒
REAL-WORLD COMMERCE

Buy something in real life and it appears in your in-game home. Buy something in the game — including food deliveries — and it arrives at your real address. The boundary between the physical and digital dissolves completely.

🌐
LEY LINES & SACRED SITES

Ley lines are visible on your AR map as glowing energy corridors — vibration runs higher along them, energy recharges faster and rare quests unlock only here. Yoga centres, temples, stone circles and ancient sacred sites all appear as power nodes. Standing on one in real life gives you a genuine advantage in-game.
